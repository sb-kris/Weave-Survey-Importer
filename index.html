    4Get the prompt

    Copy our prompt into Claude or ChatGPT, then attach your questionnaire (PDF, Word, Excel or CSV) or describe the survey you want. It replies with your survey in the format Weave reads. If you have your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini key, you can format it here instead.

    Question types

    • Short text · Long text · Email · Phone · Number · URL · Date
    • Single choice · Multiple choice · Dropdown · Yes/No
    • Rating · Opinion scale · Matrix · Bipolar matrix
    • Rank order · Constant sum · Slider
    • File upload · Image upload · Audio upload · Signature · Consent

    Converted in ClassicForm surveys

    • NPS is created as Opinion scale
    • CSAT is created as Rating
    • CES is created as Rating

    Survey structure

    • Sections
    • Welcome text
    • Thank-you text
    • Question descriptions
    • Simple display logic, like "Show if: Q3 equals Yes"

    Not supported yet

    • AI inside the app
    • PDF/Word upload inside the app
    • Themes · Channels · Sharing · Dashboards
    • Advanced branching · Response import · Complex logic
    • Full migration from other tools

    Format your survey with Claude or ChatGPT

    They rewrite your questionnaire into the format Weave reads. Four steps:

    Classic Form prompt
    1. 1
      Copy the prompt

      This prompt is for Classic Form surveys. For a different type, close this, change the survey type in step 3, and open it again.

    2. 2
      Open a new chat

      Use whichever you prefer.

      Open Claude Open ChatGPT
    3. 3
      Ask it to turn your survey into Weave's format

      Paste the prompt into the chat. Then attach your questionnaire (PDF, Word, Excel or CSV), or describe the survey you want, and add a line asking it to format the survey. For example:

      Use the instructions above to turn my survey into the Weave import format. Reply with only the formatted survey.

      If the reply isn't a list of questions starting with Survey Title: and Q1., ask it to "use the exact format from the prompt" and try again.

    4. 4
      Bring the reply back here

      Copy the whole reply and paste it into step 5, Paste your survey.

    Using variables? Write them however you like, for example $customer_name or {{customer_name}}. Weave reads both.

    Clear all variables?

    This will remove all variables added in this session.
